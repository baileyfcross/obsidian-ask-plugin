# Algebraic Term

An algebraic term is a component of an [[Algebraic Expression]] separated from other terms by outermost addition or subtraction. It can contain a number, variables, exponents, and products, such as (-4a^2b^3).

The sign belongs to the term, so the expression (a-3b) can be read as the sum of (a) and (-3b). A term's numerical factor is its [[Numerical Coefficient]]. Terms can be combined only when their variable factors and corresponding exponents match, making them [[Like Terms]].
