# Algebraic Word Problem

An algebraic word problem describes quantitative relationships in ordinary language and asks for an unknown value. Solving begins by assigning a variable, expressing every related quantity in terms of it, and translating the stated relationship into an [[Equation]] or inequality.

The symbolic manipulation is only the middle of the process. Units and context guide the model, and the numerical solution must be interpreted and checked against the original situation. Consecutive-number, age, cost, rate, mixture, and sharing problems differ in story but use the same cycle of representation, solution, and verification.
