# Algorithm Effectiveness

Algorithm effectiveness means that every prescribed step is elementary enough to be carried out exactly in a reasonable amount of time. The method must say what action to perform rather than depend on intuition, revelation, or an operation that cannot actually be completed.

Effectiveness at the step level does not guarantee that the whole [[Algorithm]] is practical. A finite procedure may still require an enormous number of effective steps, which is why [[Computational Complexity]] is evaluated separately.
